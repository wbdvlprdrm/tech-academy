What is
Tech Academy?

A practical technology learning platform for people who want to build real skills and understand how technology works.

We’re starting with fundamentals like Linux, Networking, Cloud, Git, Docker and DevOps, then building from there. The path is shaped around real learner needs and hands-on practice.

We’re not filling a catalog with courses for the sake of it. We’re listening to people who want to learn—and using that feedback to shape the first programs.

From call center
to tech.

I know what it’s like to have English, professional experience, and the desire to move forward—but not know exactly what skill to learn next.

I spent years working in an English-speaking call center in El Salvador, serving customers in the US. I was earning around $600 a month and wanted to build skills that could add more value to my professional profile. That led me to start learning technology: Linux, Cloud, Networking, and a lot of figuring things out along the way.

Tech Academy comes from that experience. The goal isn’t to promise an easy career change. It’s to offer a practical place to start, a clearer path to follow, and skills you can continue building.

07 HONEST LEARNING, REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

No false promises.

Learning technology does not guarantee a job, a specific salary, or a career change. Your results depend on your effort, experience, projects, opportunities, and the job market.

We’d rather help you build something real than sell you a dream.
